# KUEST design research

## References

- [Cowboy](https://cowboy.com/): product-first storytelling, restrained copy, strong editorial rhythm and a clear invitation to test ride.
- [VanMoof](https://www.vanmoof.com/): design-led product presentation and lifestyle framing around city riding.
- [NIU](https://www.niu.com/en/about): urban mobility positioned through technology, style and freedom.
- [Segway Motors](https://motors.segway.com/np/): category clarity across electric mobility products.
- [Prime Ride](/home/rcoirini/proyectos-bs/prime-ride) and [Nuvola Home](/home/rcoirini/proyectos-bs/nuvola-home): local references for reusable components, responsive structure, catalog data and restrained reveal motion.

## Decisions for KUEST

- A dark, cinematic hero gives the logo and real product imagery immediate presence.
- The page alternates dark graphite and warm paper sections to create rhythm instead of a generic card grid.
- Electric cyan is reserved for the lightning mark, key words, navigation cues and CTAs.
- Product data is separated in `src/data/products.ts`; local product photography is the source of truth for names, categories and prices.
- Lifestyle imagery is used only to communicate the human context of urban riding; it does not claim to depict a specific KUEST model.
- Motion is limited to hero float and image hover scale, with a reduced-motion media query.
